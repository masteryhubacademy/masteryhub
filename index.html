Frequently Asked Questions

Are these official university degrees?

No. MasteryHub programs are independent educational programs and are not official university degrees, licenses or professional certifications.

Can I request a different language?

Yes. Select your preferred language when ordering. We are expanding language availability for worldwide learners.

How do I order a course?

Choose a course, click Order Now, enter your information and preferred language, then submit the order.

How does payment work?

Orders are handled manually. Payment instructions are provided after the order is received, and payment is independently verified before delivery.

Can I study from any country?

MasteryHub is designed for worldwide learners. Availability of specific payment and delivery options may vary by location.

Do you ask for passwords or OTP codes?

No. Never send passwords, OTP codes, seed phrases, private keys or other sensitive security information.
